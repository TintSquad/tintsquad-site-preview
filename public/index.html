FAQ

Quick answers

Can I roll my windows down after tint?

Leave your windows rolled up for at least 48 hours after the film is installed so it can set.

What are the tint laws in New York?

For most passenger vehicles, New York requires front side windows to let in at least 70% of light, and the windshield can only have non-reflective tint on the top 6 inches. On SUVs, pickup trucks and vans, the rear side windows and rear window can be darker; on sedans, the rear side windows also have to meet the 70% rule. Limited medical exemptions exist. Laws change, so check the NY DMV for the current rules before choosing a shade, and ask us if you have questions.

What film do you use?

LLumar, a dyed automotive film, in 5%, 15%, 20% and 35%. It's backed by a lifetime warranty against manufacturer defects like bubbling, peeling, fading or color change.

Why don't you carry ceramic tint?

We've built our shop around one film we trust and stand behind: LLumar. Ceramic is a different, higher-priced product line that we simply don't offer. If ceramic is a must-have for you, another shop may be a better fit.

Does the shade percentage affect the price?

No. Your shade choice doesn't change the price.
